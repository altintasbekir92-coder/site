## Soufflé Recipe


<div style="font-family: 'Georgia', serif; font-size: 1.1em; color: #333; line-height: 1.6;">

### 🍫 Ingredients

* **110g** Dark Chocolate *(60-70% cocoa)*
* **4** Egg Yolks
* **5** Egg Whites
* **55g** Sugar
* **1 tbsp** Flour
* **2 tbsp** Butter *(melted)*

---

> *Extra butter and sugar for greasing the ramekins.*

</div>


<div style="font-family: 'Georgia', serif; font-size: 1.1em; line-height: 1.6; color: #2c3e50;">

## 👨‍🍳 Instructions

1.  **Preheat:** Set your oven to **180-200°C** (350-400°F). Grease the ramekins with butter using *upward strokes* and coat them evenly with sugar.
2.  **Melt:** Melt the chocolate and butter together using a **double boiler** (bain-marie) until smooth.
3.  **Whisk:** Whisk the egg whites with sugar until **stiff peaks** form (the stiff meringue stage).
4.  **Combine:** Stir the egg yolks and flour into the melted chocolate mixture.
5.  **Fold:** Gently fold the whisked egg whites into the chocolate mixture using a spatula. *Be careful not to deflate them!*
6.  **Bake:** Divide the mixture into the ramekins and bake for **8-10 minutes**.

---

### 💡 Tips and Tricks

* **Texture:** If you overcook it, it becomes a cake; if you undercook it, the center stays raw. **10 minutes** is usually the sweet spot.
* **Rise:** Adding a pinch of salt or cream of tartar while whisking the egg whites helps them hold their shape.
* **Greasing:** Brushing the butter in **upward motions** helps the soufflé "climb" the sides of the dish as it bakes.
